📱 Pickup Code Feature
───────────────────────
Idea struck when my parents were arguing:
  • Father: "Write a list, I'll go to the store"
  • Mother: "Just go and call me what you need"

I thought — why can't mother just order from home,
and father just pick up? No list, no phone calls.

→ That's how the pickup code was born.
  Mom orders online → dad shows the code at store → grabs it in 1 min.

👥 Shared Cart & Bill Splitting
─────────────────────────────────
Same thinking extended to roommates and families:
  1 person can't track everyone's items → Shared cart
  Everyone adds what they need → one bill → auto split

→ Solves roommates' biggest headache:
  "Who bought what? Who owes whom?"
