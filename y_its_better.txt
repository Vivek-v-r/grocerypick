"Large apps are great for home delivery, but small stores can't
afford 30% commissions or manage delivery fleets. My system lets
a local grocery store go online for pickup — customers pre-order,
store prepares it, customer walks in and grabs it in 1 minute.
No commission, no delivery cost, no waiting."
